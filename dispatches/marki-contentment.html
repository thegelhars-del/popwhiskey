By Order of the Committee · Duly Entered into the Great Ledger

The Contentment
of Mr. Marki

Three years a pledge. Not one enquiry. No complaint, no petition, no visible distress of any kind. The Society has examined this from every angle available to it and must now report that it cannot get over it.

Filed by Mr. Edmund Vance · A Standing Matter

The reader is asked to sit down. What follows is not an infraction, which is precisely what makes it insupportable.

Mr. Payton Marki, originally of Missouri and presently of Milwaukee, has been a pledge of the Pop Whiskey Society of Wisconsin since the year 2023.

Three years.

In that time he has not petitioned. He has not enquired. He has not, by any account this Correspondent has been able to gather from any quarter, so much as raised the subject of his own candidacy in the hearing of a member in good standing. He holds the rank of Secret Probationary — the middle of the three tiers, arrived at by a process which has never been explained to him and about which he has never asked.

And he appears to be perfectly happy.

The Society Wishes to Be Clear About the Charge

There is no charge. That is the difficulty.

This Correspondent has filed upon a man for the contents of his golf bag. This Correspondent has filed upon a proposal concerning outerwear. This Correspondent has, on one occasion the Society would rather not revisit, filed upon a garnish. There is no conduct so small that it cannot be entered into the record if the record is approached in the proper spirit.

Mr. Marki has given this Correspondent nothing.

He is pleasant. He is well regarded. He is, when he attends, exactly the sort of man the Society was assembled to contain. And he has spent three years in a condition that every other aspirant in the Society’s history has treated as an emergency, and he has treated it as a Tuesday.

The Aspirancy Page Warned Us

Let the reader consult the Society’s published page upon Aspirancy, where it is set down in plain language what a candidate ought not to expect: a timeline; a status update; a response issued at his own request; acknowledgement that a process is, in fact, underway.

That passage was written as a caution. It was written to prepare a man for a difficult wait.

Mr. Marki appears to have read it as a set of instructions and followed them to the letter for thirty-six months.

This Correspondent has come to believe — and states it plainly, and will not be softening it — that the Society has finally produced a candidate it cannot outlast.

The Aggravating Comparison

The Correspondent would not ordinarily introduce a second gentleman into a filing of this nature. The Correspondent is going to do it anyway.

Mr. Jordan Steltenpohl was recognised upon the first of May of this year and was wearing the jacket by the eleventh of July. Seventy-one days. He is entered upon the Membership as an Associate Member and the Commodore has said aloud that he expects great things of him.

Mr. Marki has been at this since 2023.

The Correspondent sets the two figures beside one another and declines to comment further, having discovered that the figures are quite capable of conducting themselves.

A Mitigating Circumstance, Entered Reluctantly

It has been brought to the Correspondent’s attention — late, and by a member who ought to have mentioned it sooner — that Mr. Marki is to be married upon the tenth of October.

The Society extends every good wish, sincerely and without reservation. A wedding is a serious business and a man is entitled to be consumed by it.

It explains the preoccupation.

It does not explain 2024. It does not explain 2025. It does not explain the golf outing from which he absented himself while in possession of a functioning calendar. The Correspondent has weighed the mitigating circumstance with the gravity it deserves and filed it accordingly, which is to say beneath the other three years.

The Correspondent further observes, without drawing any conclusion whatever, that the Society’s delegation departs for Ohio upon the eighteenth of October — being eight days after the wedding, and being ample time for a man to have returned from wherever a wedding sends him.

The matter has gone before the Council, as such matters do. The Correspondent has heard nothing since and has stopped asking.

Congratulations, Mr. Marki. Sincerely. Now for the love of all that is holy, ask us about your standing.

✦ A Proclamation From the Commodore

I have been asked to account for Mr. Marki’s standing and I find that I cannot.

It has been open to him, at any hour of any of these three years, to press his case. He has not pressed it. He has never once asked. I have waited for him to ask in the way a man waits for a kettle, and the kettle has simply declined to boil, and I have come to respect it.

Let it be recorded that he is to be married upon the tenth of October, and that this Society wishes him every happiness in it. Let it be recorded separately that a wedding in October accounts for an October, and that the Great Ledger contains three years.

Mr. Marki: the Society is not, at this time, prepared to advance a man who has not indicated he wishes to be advanced. Indicate it. That is the whole of the obstacle, and it has been the whole of it since 2023.

Peter Gelhar Commodore · Pop Whiskey Society of Wisconsin
