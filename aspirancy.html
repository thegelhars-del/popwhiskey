On Aspirancy

The Pop Whiskey Society of Wisconsin is not, and shall never be, a club one joins. It is a society into which one is, in the fullness of time and at the sole discretion of the standing membership, invited.

We mention this at the outset to spare all parties the discomfort of misunderstanding.

A Privilege, Most Emphatically Not a Right

Membership is conferred. It is not earned through application, lobbied for through correspondence, or accelerated by enthusiasm — however genuine that enthusiasm may be. The Society reserves, in perpetuity, the absolute right to extend or withhold an invitation for any reason, no reason, or reasons it does not see fit to disclose. This is not caprice. It is the quiet machinery by which the Society has, since 2019, preserved that particular character which our membership has come to expect and our guests have come to appreciate.

The Pledge Process

Aspiring pledges may rest assured that a process exists. They may further rest assured that it is observed with rigour. Beyond this, the particulars are matters for the Society and its initiates — not for the broader public, nor for the candidate himself until such time as the Commodore deems disclosure appropriate.

What a candidate may reasonably expect:

  • A period of acquaintance, the duration of which is not fixed and shall not be discussed.
  • Observation. Quiet, considered, and from multiple quarters.
  • Strict adherence to the standards of conduct, dress, and decorum that govern all Society functions — observed not as a formality of the pledge, but as a condition of consideration.
  • Eventual word, delivered through the proper channels, regarding the disposition of his candidacy.

What a candidate ought not to expect:

  • A timeline.
  • A status update.
  • A response of any kind issued at the candidate's request rather than the Society's initiative.
  • Acknowledgement that a process is, in fact, underway.

On Communication

All matters pertaining to candidacy — including, where appropriate, the very existence of a candidacy — are communicated solely by the Commodore, or disseminated through the proper hierarchical channels of the Society. Inquiries directed elsewhere will not be answered. Inquiries directed to the Commodore will be answered when, and only when, the Commodore is so inclined.

This is not unkindness. It is the order of things.

A Closing Word

Those who find these terms disagreeable have, in that very disagreement, supplied the Society with all the information it requires. Those who find them entirely reasonable — even, perhaps, rather sensible — may take some quiet comfort in the fact.

Vice Commodore

Matthew Gelhar

Vice Commodore & Co-Founding Member · Pop Whiskey Society of Wisconsin

Every great institution has its origin story, and the Pop Whiskey Society of Wisconsin is no different. At the crossroads of Terrebonne Drive and County Road X, where all good things begin, it was Matthew Gelhar — younger brother, co-conspirator, and the second signature on the sacred charter — who helped breathe life into what would become one of Wausau's most distinguished brotherhoods.

As Vice Commodore, Matthew occupies a singular and somewhat mythological position within the Society. He does not attend many gatherings. He has not attended most gatherings. There are newer members who have never laid eyes on the man, and who have quietly begun to wonder whether he exists at all — whether "Matthew Gelhar" is perhaps an honorific, a legend, a ghost who haunts the bylaws.

He is not a ghost. He is a founder. And that distinction, within this Society, is everything. His chair remains set at every meeting. His pour remains accounted for. And should he ever choose to grace us with his presence, the Society will rise — glasses raised — as one.

His dram of choice, when he deigns to appear: Lagavulin 16 — smoke, depth, and a finish that lingers long after the glass is empty. Much like the man himself.

A Word From the Founder

The Commodore's Corner

The Commodore

Peter Gelhar

Commodore & Founding Member · Est. 2019 · Wausau, Wisconsin

Friends, gentlemen, and honored guests —

Welcome to the Pop Whiskey Society of Wisconsin.

I'll be honest with you — and honesty, much like a fine dram, is something we hold in high regard here. This whole thing started as an idea. One of many, if you ask anyone who knows me. I have ideas the way Wisconsin has winters: frequently, with varying degrees of severity, and occasionally leaving those around me wondering what exactly they've gotten themselves into.

This one, though. This one worked.

What began in 2019 at the crossroads of Terrebonne Drive and County Road X — with a bottle, a charter, and a handful of gentlemen willing to take a chance on something — has grown into a brotherhood I am genuinely proud of. Over fifty members strong. A motto that means something. Events that people actually show up to. Even a website, apparently.

We are not a drinking club. We are a Society. There is a difference, and it matters to us. We gather to learn, to share, to argue respectfully about whether Islay or Speyside deserves the crown, and to remind ourselves that the best moments in life are the ones shared with good people over something worth savoring.

So whether you're a member in good standing, a guest of a member, or simply someone who stumbled upon this page through some mysterious alignment of fate and search algorithms — you are welcome here. Pour yourself something worthy. Stay awhile.

In Aqua Vitae Veritas,

Peter Gelhar
Commodore, Pop Whiskey Society of Wisconsin
