Gentlemen of the Society — and those pledges fortunate enough to have been deemed worthy of consideration — your Commodore is pleased beyond measure to announce the return of The Great Northern Open, the crown jewel of the PWSOW competitive calendar and what amounts, in this Commodore’s estimation, to the Masters of Central Wisconsin.

Twenty-four spots have been reserved for the worthy. The vast majority are already spoken for. Procrastinators shall find themselves spectators. Registration is conducted, as has been our custom, via the link below — fees paid in advance, that the day itself may remain uncluttered by commerce.

The Format — Under deliberation by the Elder Council. Updates shall be issued in due course. This is intentional, not lazy.
The Teams — Assembled by the Commodore, serving as Puppet Master, in noble pursuit of fairness — to the best of his abilities, which, given the membership’s wildly varying skill levels among the Society’s knuckleheads and honeyucks, will fall short of perfection. He will, nevertheless, do his best.
The Dress — Pine Valley imposes no dress code, being a public course rather than a country club. Course attire is at your discretion. The post-round festivities demand the tweed sport coat. Bring the jacket. This is not a request. Pledges, in their pre-jacketed state, are excused.
Pledges — Non-members deemed worthy of consideration by current members are welcome to attend and compete.
