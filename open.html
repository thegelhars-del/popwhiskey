Official Rules of Play

I.Format

Four-person scramble. Eighteen holes. Five teams.

II.Tee Shots

All four players tee off on every hole. The team selects the best ball and plays from there.

III.Tees

Men play from the silver tees. Women play from the red tees.

IV.Playing the Selected Ball

Mark the selected ball’s spot. Each player plays from within one club length of the mark — no closer to the hole, and staying in the same condition. Rough stays rough; sand stays sand.

V.The Two-Putt Rule

Once the team’s selected ball is on the green, each player gets one putt from the selected ball’s spot. If any player holes it, it counts. If no one makes it, the team records one additional putt — a two-putt — and moves to the next hole. No second putts are taken.

VI.Mulligans

Each player receives one mulligan for the round. Honor system. A mulligan may be used on any shot from tee to green, but never on the putting green — the Two-Putt Rule stands inviolate.

VII.Scoring

Record one team score per hole. Maximum score on any hole is double bogey — once reached, pick up, record it, and move on. Lowest eighteen-hole total wins the Great Northern Open and the Jacket of Distinction.

Ties are broken by scorecard playoff: best back-nine total, then the last six holes, then the last three, then the 18th alone.

VIII.Pace of Play

Keep up with the group ahead. Beer may be consumed at pace; golf must be played at pace.

IX.Spectator Carts

Beer cart and spectator crew are support personnel. They may encourage, heckle, and hydrate, but may not strike a ball or advise a putt.

X.Contests

Skill contests are in play. Holes and prizes will be announced the day of the event.

XI.Rulings

All disputes are settled on the spot by the Commodore. All rulings are final and shall be entered into the Great Ledger.

The matter is therefore considered settled.

On Conduct

The Great Northern Open is contested for fun, for fellowship, and — of course — for the trophy. Assholery, in any and all forms, shall not be tolerated.

Save it for Sunday.

The After Party

In a departure from previous years, the Society shall remain at Pine Valley for the evening’s celebration. A covered pavilion awaits — tables and chairs provided, two grills at the ready.

The menu is subject to change, but expect brisket sandwiches alongside a proper spread of sides.

Vegetarians are advised to remain home. It is not that type of club, for God’s sake.

✦

The Jacket of Distinction

All Brethren shall bring their Jackets of Distinction. Owing to the forecasted weather and the open-air nature of the evening’s festivities, the wearing of them is hereby declared optional — donned briefly, perhaps, for the official photograph. The Society is confident its members will be sufficiently fragrant by day’s end without the assistance of insulated wool.

Those dedicated few who wear theirs regardless shall be awarded extra points, duly entered into the Great Ledger. What the points may be redeemed for, the Ledger declines to say.
