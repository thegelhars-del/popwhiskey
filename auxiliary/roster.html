The Ladies’ Auxiliary

The Standing Offices

The offices stand ready, their Ladies not yet named.

The Chief Offices
The First Lady
Conferred upon her who presides over the Auxiliary and all its works, and whose hand the Society sees in every undertaking that does not collapse.
Awaiting her Ladyship
Her Ladyship the Loyal Opposition
Conferred upon her who regards the whole enterprise with a skeptic’s eye and finds it wanting. Hers is the sanctioned voice of dissent; she is under no obligation to approve of the Society, and is charged, indeed, never to.
Awaiting her Ladyship
The Lady of the Hearth
Conferred upon her beneath whose roof the Society gathers and by whose welcome it is sustained, honored for as long as the fire burns.
Awaiting her Ladyship
Her Ladyship in Good Standing
Conferred upon her who holds no claim upon the Society but that she is beloved of it — a member in all but the one particular the wall forbids.
Awaiting her Ladyship
Further Offices of the Auxiliary
The Empress Dowager
Conferred upon her who rules, as the great empresses always have, from behind a curtain — the throne being occupied by a man who believes it is his.
Awaiting her Ladyship
The Chatelaine
Conferred upon her who holds the keys to the castle. Every door, every lock, every cabinet — including the one where the good whiskey is kept, the location of which the gentleman has never once guessed.
Awaiting her Ladyship
The Lady Patroness
Conferred upon her who decides who is received and who is not. The gentlemen keep a Wall of Shame; she keeps a list they have never seen, and pray never to.
Awaiting her Ladyship
The Mother Superior
Conferred upon her whose authority is the model for all lesser orders, including this one. The title states the Society’s official finding in the matter: she is the mother of the house, and she is superior.
Awaiting her Ladyship
The Matron
Conferred upon her who commands the infirmary on the morning after and the household at all other hours. Generations of gentlemen have feared the Matron on sight. The instinct is sound.
Awaiting her Ladyship
The Mistress of the Purse
Conferred upon her who issues the allowance, audits the whiskey budget, and closes the books with a rigor no federal service has approached.
Awaiting her Ladyship
The Diarist
Conferred upon her who keeps the true history, having been present, sober, and paying attention. The gentlemen’s accounts improve with each telling; hers remains inconveniently exact. Her diary is the only publication the Society fears.
Awaiting her Ladyship
The Headmistress
Conferred upon her who governs conduct, corrects posture, and confiscates what is being misused. Detention is real. Her office door is always open; her patience is not.
Awaiting her Ladyship
The Housemother
Conferred upon her who keeps the house rules and enforces the curfew, and who has seen, catalogued, and forgiven everything the gentlemen believe they got away with.
Awaiting her Ladyship
The Lady-in-Waiting
Conferred upon her who has waited: in the car, in the foyer, at the table for two, at the door at midnight. The office is ancient. The waiting is eternal.
Awaiting her Ladyship
The Mistress of the Robes
Conferred upon her who dresses the gentleman, who would otherwise appear in public in the first three things he touched. Nothing leaves the house unapproved. Nothing.
Awaiting her Ladyship
The Godmother
Conferred upon her to whom all favors trace and all respects are paid. She has never had to ask twice.
Awaiting her Ladyship

These offices shall not stand vacant forever.

Do you fancy yourself a Lady of this Auxiliary? Seats are limited. Thrones are few. The gentlemen are, frankly, desperate. Text the Commodore directly — and do it NOW. He shall not be hard to find for a lady of true ambition. Positions are filled in the order the Society sees fit, and hesitation is the enemy of ambition. Don’t delay. Don’t dither. Secure your title before another claims it.

(Operators — that is, the Commodore — standing by. His number is not printed here; a resourceful Lady will manage.)
