Some 640 miles from Columbus, Ohio to the first tee at Pine Valley. Which brother wants it more? · the Eleventh of July

✦ How This Came to Pass ✦

The precise origin of the matter has, like all great feuds, been polished smooth by retelling — but the Society's archivists are agreed upon its essential shape. Two brothers, both Kavanagh, both held in the highest regard by this Society, fell to debating a question as old as brotherhood itself: which of them would actually make it.

Each, it is said, professed perfect confidence in his own attendance — and a brother's affectionate doubt about the other's. Brother Thomas answers to no employer but himself, and therefore to the most merciless taskmaster of all: his own calendar. Brother John's standing at a great national concern keeps him forever one summons away from a flight in the wrong direction. Some six hundred and forty miles of northbound highway lie between boast and proof.

A lesser society would have let the matter rest. This is not a lesser society. Where two men of consequence stake their honour on a question of devotion, the Society is duty-bound to convene, to tally the odds, and — God willing and the calendars permitting — to welcome them both to the table at the Great Northern Open. The wager is open. The Commodore is watching.
